STUDY STRATEGY

A practical approach to studying finance

01

Understand the concept

Start by understanding what a financial concept measures and why it matters.

02

Learn the formula

Understand the components of the formula rather than memorising symbols without context.

03

Practise calculations

Apply the formula to realistic financial figures and check your calculations.

04

Interpret the result

Explain what the result means for the company's financial performance or position.

05

Compare results

Compare results across periods or against appropriate benchmarks.

06

Build your conclusion

Combine the evidence into a clear financial analysis and conclusion.
