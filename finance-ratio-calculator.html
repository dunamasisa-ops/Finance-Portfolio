FINANCIAL RATIOS

What is a finance ratio calculator?

A finance ratio calculator helps you calculate relationships between figures in financial statements. These calculations can provide useful information about a company's financial performance and position.

Financial ratios are commonly grouped into liquidity, profitability, solvency and efficiency categories.

EzpoHub provides practical finance resources designed to help students and professionals calculate and interpret financial ratios more effectively.

RATIO CATEGORIES

Common financial ratios

These ratios are commonly used when analysing financial statements.

LIQUIDITY

Current Ratio

Measures the relationship between current assets and current liabilities.

Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities
PROFITABILITY

Net Profit Margin

Measures the percentage of revenue remaining as net profit.

Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100
SOLVENCY

Debt-to-Equity Ratio

Compares debt financing with shareholders' equity.

Total Debt ÷ Shareholders' Equity
PROFITABILITY

Return on Equity

Indicates how effectively shareholders' equity is used to generate profit.

Net Income ÷ Equity × 100
